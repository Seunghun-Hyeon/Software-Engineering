**Handong ClubHub**

User Manual

#

# **1\. Introduction**

Handong ClubHub is a web-based club management platform designed for Handong Global University (HGU) students and club leaders. The platform allows students to discover clubs, view club profiles, save events, and apply to join clubs. Club executives can manage their club profile, post events, review applications, and manage their members all in one place.

## **1.1 Who Is This Manual For?**

This manual is intended for two types of users:

• Students \- HGU students who want to explore clubs, apply for membership, and track their club activities.

• Club Executives (Managers) \- Club leaders who manage their club profile, events, applications, and members.

# **2\. Getting Started**

## **2.1 Creating an Account**

To create a new account on ClubHub, follow these steps:

1. Go to the ClubHub homepage and click the Sign Up button in the top right corner of the header.
2. Fill in your details on the registration form:
   1. First Name
   2. Last Name
   3. University Email (must be @handong.ac.kr or @[handong.edu](http://handong.edu))
   4. Password
3. Click the Sign Up button to create your account.
4. You will be redirected to the homepage after successful registration.

## **2.2 Logging In**

To log in to your existing account:

1. Click the Log In button in the top right corner of the header.
2. Enter your university email address and password.
3. Click Sign In.
4. If you are a regular student, you will be redirected to the homepage.
5. If you are a club executive, you will see a role selection screen where you can choose to continue as a Student or as an Executive.

## **2.3 Signing Out**

To sign out of your account, click the Sign Out button in the header. You will be redirected to the homepage after signing out.

# **3\. For Students**

## **3.1 Browsing Clubs**

To explore clubs available at HGU:

1. Click Clubs in the navigation header.
2. You will see a list of all clubs with their name, category, and a brief description.
3. Use the search bar to search for a specific club by name. You must enter at least 3 characters for the search to activate.
4. Use the category filter buttons to filter clubs by category such as Sports, Academic, Christian Groups, Performing Arts, and more.
5. Click View Details on any club card to see the full club profile.

## **3.2 Viewing a Club Profile**

A club profile page contains the following information:

• Club name, logo, and cover image

• Club description and mission

• Category

• Meeting schedule and location

• Membership fee

• Social links (Instagram, KakaoTalk, YouTube)

• Leadership team

• News and articles posted by the club

• Upcoming events

• Photo and video gallery

## **3.3 Following a Club**

To follow a club and save it to your profile:

1. Go to the club's profile page.
2. Click the Follow button near the top of the page.
3. The button will change to Following to confirm you are now following the club.
4. To unfollow, click the Following button again.

Note: You must be logged in to follow a club. If you are not logged in, you will be prompted to log in or sign up.

## **3.4 Applying to a Club**

To apply to join a club that is currently accepting applications:

1. Go to the club's profile page.
2. Click the Join Club button. This button is only active when the club is accepting applications.
3. If the club is not currently accepting applications, a message will appear stating so.
4. Fill in the application form with the required answers.
5. Click Submit Application.
6. Your application will be sent to the club manager for review.

Note: The application form is customized by each club. Questions may vary from club to club.

## **3.5 Saving Events**

To save an event to your profile:

1. Go to the Events page from the navigation header.
2. Browse upcoming events.
3. Click the Save button on any event card to save it to your profile.

## **3.6 Student Dashboard**

Your student dashboard is accessible by clicking your name in the header. It contains the following tabs:

**Saved Events**

View all events you have saved. Click on an event to see its full details.

**Favourite Clubs**

View all clubs you are currently following.

**My Applications**

View the status of all club applications you have submitted. You can see whether your application is Pending or Accepted.

**My Clubs**

View all clubs you are currently a member of, along with your role in each club.

**Settings**

Edit your profile information including your first name and last name. You can also delete your account from the Danger Zone section.

# **4\. For Club Executives**

## **4.1 Accessing the Executive Account**

If you have been assigned as a club executive by the system administrator:

1.  Log in with your university email and password.
2.  A role selection screen will appear. Click Continue to Executive to access the manager dashboard.
3.  If you are already logged in as a student, click your name in the header to open the drop down menu.
4.  Click Manager Dashboard to switch to the executive account

Note: You must be assigned as an executive in the system before you can access the manager dashboard. Contact your system administrator if you believe you should have executive access.

## **4.2 Editing the Club Profile**

To update your club's public profile:

1. Go to the manager dashboard and click the Settings tab.
2. The form will be pre-filled with your club's current information.
3. Edit any of the following fields:
   1. Club name and category
   2. Description
   3. Info (history, mission, and goals)
   4. Core values
   5. Meeting schedule and location
   6. Membership fee
   7. Social links (Instagram, KakaoTalk, YouTube)
   8. Leadership team (add multiple leaders with name, role, and photo)
   9. Gallery photos and videos
   10. News articles
4. Click Save Changes when you are done.

Note: Changes saved here will be reflected on the public club profile that all students can view.

## **4.3 Managing Members**

To view and manage your club members:

1. Go to the manager dashboard and click the Members tab.
2. You will see a list of all current members with their name, role, and join date.

**Adding a New Member**

1. Click the Add Member button at the top of the page.
2. Enter the university email address of the student you want to add.
3. Click Search to look up the account.
4. If the account is found, their name, and email will be displayed.
5. Select a club role and join date.
6. Click Add to Club to confirm.

**Editing a Member**

1. Click the Edit button next to a member's name.
2. You can update their Club Role and Join Date.
3. Click Save Changes to confirm.

Note: You cannot edit a member's personal information such as their name or email Only club-related fields can be edited.

**Removing a Member**

1. Click the Remove button next to a member's name.
2. Confirm the removal in the confirmation dialog.

## **4.4 Managing Applications and Recruitment**

The Applications & Recruitment tab has two inner tabs:

**Application Form Builder**

To create and publish your club's application form:

1. Click the Application Form Builder tab.
2. Use the Accept Applications toggle to open or close applications. When toggled on, students can see the Join Club button on your club profile and submit applications.
3. Build your application form by adding fields:
   1. Short Answer \- a single line text input
   2. Long Answer \- a multi-line text area
   3. Multiple Choice \- a set of options for the applicant to choose from
   4. File Upload \- allows the applicant to upload a file
4. For each field, enter a question label and set whether it is required.
5. Click Publish Form when your form is ready. Students will now see your custom form when they apply.

**Applicant Tracking**

To review submitted applications:

1. Click the Applicant Tracking tab.
2. You will see two columns: Pending and Accepted.
3. Click View Application on any pending application to see the applicant's full answers.
4. To accept an applicant, click Accept Applicant. They will be moved to the Accepted column and automatically added as a club member.
5. To reject an applicant, click Reject. Their application will be deleted and they will be notified.

## **4.5 Managing Events**

To manage your club's events, go to the Events tab. It has three inner tabs:

**Create Event**

To post a new event:

1.  Click the Create Event tab.
2.  Fill in the event details:
    1.  Event name
    2.  Date and time
    3.  Location
    4.  Description and overview
    5.  Photos and videos
    6.  Requires tickets toggle
3.  Click Publish Event. The event will appear on the public Events page that all students can view.

**Upcoming Events**

View all events that have not yet taken place. You can edit or delete any upcoming event.

**Past Events**

View all events that have already taken place. You can view the details of past events.

## **4.6 Transferring Club Ownership**

To transfer club leadership to another member:

1. Go to the Settings tab in the manager dashboard.
2. Click the Danger Zone tab
3. Under Transfer Ownership, select the member you want to transfer leadership to from the dropdown.
4. Confirm the transfer.
5. The selected member will become the new club executive. You will remain a Regular Member of the club but will lose executive access.

Note: This action cannot be undone. Make sure you select the correct member before confirming.

# **5\. Switching Between Student and Executive Accounts**

If you are a club executive, you have access to both a student account and an executive account. You can switch between them without signing out:

• When in executive mode, click Switch to Student Account in the side menu to return to the student experience.

• When in student mode, click your name in the header to open the drop down menu and click Manager Dashboard to switch to the executive account

# **6\. Account Settings**

## **6.1 Editing Your Profile**

To edit your personal information:

1. Click your name in the header.
2. Go to the Settings tab.
3. Update your First Name or Last Name.
4. Click Save Changes.

## **6.2 Deleting Your Account**

To permanently delete your account:

1. Go to the Settings tab in your student dashboard.
2. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
3. Click Delete Account.
4. Confirm the deletion in the confirmation dialog.
5. Your account will be permanently deleted and you will be signed out.

Note: Account deletion is permanent. You will not be able to log in again after deleting your account. If you want to use ClubHub again, you will need to create a new account.

# **7\. Troubleshooting**

## **7.1 I cannot log in**

• Make sure you are using your university email address (@handong.ac.kr or @handong.edu).

• Double check that your password is correct.

• If you have forgotten your password, click Forgot Password on the login page.

## **7.2 I cannot see the Executive option after logging in**

• You may not have been assigned as a club executive yet. Contact your system administrator.

• Make sure your account has been linked to a club in the system.

## **7.3 The Join Club button is not working**

• The club may not be currently accepting applications. Check if the button says the club is not accepting members.

• Make sure you are logged in before trying to apply.

## **7.4 My changes are not showing up**

• Try refreshing the page after saving changes.

• Some changes may take a moment to appear as they are synced with the backend server.
