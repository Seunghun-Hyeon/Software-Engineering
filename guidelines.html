Registration & Executive Status

Official requirements for maintaining active club status and verifying leadership credentials.

  • check_circle

    Semester Renewals

    All clubs must submit renewal applications at the start of every academic semester to remain active on the platform.

  • check_circle

    Executive Verification

    Leadership roles must be held by current students with valid @handong.ac.kr, @handong.edu, or HISNet credentials.

  • check_circle

    Pilot Clubs Status

    New organizations enter a 'Pilot' phase for one semester before achieving full recognition status.

Need further clarification?

If you have questions about these guidelines, need to report a violation, or require assistance with a club dispute, please reach out to the Club Administration team.
